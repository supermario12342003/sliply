Receipt vs invoice

Invoice asks for payment. Receipt confirms payment was received.

Educational only — not tax or legal advice.

Quick comparison

Invoice = amount due, payment terms, often sent before or at delivery.

Receipt = proof money was received (cash, card, transfer).

When to use which

  • Outstanding payment → invoice first.
  • Already paid (especially cash) → receipt.
  • Some tools combine both; for simple cash sales a receipt is enough.
