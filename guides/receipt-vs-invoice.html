Receipt vs invoice

People mix these up. Here is the practical difference for freelancers and small shops.

Create a receipt free →

Invoice = you are asking to be paid (amount due, payment terms).

Receipt = you confirm payment was received (proof of payment).

When to use which

  • Send an invoice before or when work is done, if payment is still outstanding.
  • Issue a receipt after cash/card/transfer is received — especially for walk-in or cash customers.
  • Some tools combine both; for simple cash sales, a receipt alone is enough.
