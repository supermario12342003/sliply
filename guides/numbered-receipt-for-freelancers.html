Numbered receipts for freelancers

A stable numbering scheme makes your books easier and looks professional. You do not need expensive software.

Educational only — not tax or legal advice.

Create a receipt free →

A simple numbering scheme

  • YYYY-0001 — year + sequence (resets each year), or
  • R-0001 — continuous sequence across years
  • Never reuse a number; skip gaps rather than renumber history

What to store

Keep PDF or print + a short ledger (date, number, client, total). Sliply can keep up to 10 free entries on this device.

France / EU note

Invoicing and e-invoicing rules are separate from simple payment receipts. If you must issue compliant invoices, follow official guidance or your accountant. Sliply is a receipt helper, not certified invoicing software.
