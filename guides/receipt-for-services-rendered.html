After the client pays for your work, issue a receipt describing the service and amount received.

What to write

  • Short description of services (dates or project name help)
  • Amount received and currency
  • Your business identity block
  • Client name
  • Receipt number + date of payment

Invoice already sent?

Reference the invoice number on the receipt if you had one (“Payment received for Invoice INV-104”).
