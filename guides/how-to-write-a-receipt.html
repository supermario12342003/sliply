How to write a receipt

A receipt is proof of payment. Keep it short, numbered, and clear — then print or save a PDF.

Educational only — not tax or legal advice.

Create a receipt free →

What every receipt should include

  • Business name (and address if you have one)
  • Date of payment
  • Unique receipt number
  • Customer or “Cash customer”
  • Line items: description, qty, price
  • Total (and tax/VAT if charged)
  • Payment method (cash, card, transfer)

EU / France tip

Registered businesses often add legal IDs (e.g. SIRET, VAT). Rules depend on your status — ask your accountant. Sliply’s “From” field is the place for those lines.

Steps

  1. Open the free generator.
  2. Fill From / To, date, number.
  3. Add line items and tax if needed.
  4. Print or PDF. Keep a copy.
