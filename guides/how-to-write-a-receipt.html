How to write a receipt

A good receipt is a short proof of payment. You do not need fancy software — just the right fields and a clear print or PDF.

Create a receipt free →

What every receipt should include

  • Business name (and address if you have one)
  • Date of the sale or payment
  • Receipt number (unique, sequential is best)
  • Customer name or “Cash customer”
  • Line items: description, quantity, unit price
  • Total (and tax / VAT if you charge it)
  • Payment method (cash, card, transfer) — optional but useful

EU / France tip

If you are a registered business, include legal identifiers where required (e.g. SIRET, VAT number). Rules vary by status — check your accountant or official guidance. Sliply lets you put those in the “From” field.

Steps

  1. Open a free generator (no signup needed).
  2. Fill From / To, date, and number.
  3. Add line items and tax if needed.
  4. Print or save as PDF. Keep a copy for your records.
