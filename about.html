About Sliply

Sliply is a lightweight receipt generator for freelancers, makers, and small shops. Create numbered receipts with line items and optional tax, print or download a PDF, and keep a short ledger — all in your browser.

Why we built it

Spreadsheet templates are slow. Heavy invoicing suites are overkill when you just need a clean slip. Sliply focuses on one job: fast, printable receipts with a private history on your device.

Privacy by design

Drafts and the ledger live in localStorage on your device. We do not require an account for the free plan. See our Privacy notice for GDPR-oriented details (France / EU).

Free vs Pro

The free plan includes a small watermark on PDFs/prints and a capped on-device ledger (10 receipts). Pro (coming soon) will unlock unlimited local history and watermark-free exports. Join the waitlist on Pricing.

Not accounting software

Sliply helps you draft receipts. It is not certified invoicing software, tax advice, or a substitute for your accountant or official e-invoicing obligations where they apply.
