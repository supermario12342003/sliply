Privacy notice

Draft for France / EU · Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Not legal advice — review with counsel before production ads or paid billing.

1. Who we are

“Sliply” is an early micro-product operated by the site publisher (contact: hello@sliply.app — placeholder until a real mailbox is configured). For GDPR purposes, the publisher is the data controller for any personal data processed via this website.

2. What the free app stores on your device

Receipt drafts, the ledger, and optional waitlist emails you submit are stored in your browser’s localStorage. That data stays on your device unless you export a PDF, print, or voluntarily email yourself (e.g. waitlist mailto). Clearing site data deletes it.

  • Receipt content you type (parties, line items, notes)
  • Timestamps and receipt numbers generated locally
  • Waitlist email + plan preference (local only, plus optional mailto you send)

3. What we do not collect (free static MVP)

This static deployment does not include a backend account system. We do not operate a Sliply database of your receipts. Hosting (e.g. Cloudflare Pages) and CDNs may process standard technical logs (IP address, user-agent, request URL, approximate location) under their own terms.

4. Cookies & advertising (planned)

Ad slots on the site are placeholders. If Google AdSense (or similar) is enabled later, third-party cookies / advertising identifiers may be used. You will need:

  • An updated privacy notice naming ad partners
  • A consent banner compliant with ePrivacy / CNIL guidance where required
  • Links to opt-out / ad preferences

Until ads are live, no AdSense scripts are loaded by this MVP.

5. Legal bases (when personal data is processed)

  • Legitimate interest / technical necessity — serving a static site, security logs at the host
  • Consent — non-essential cookies / personalized ads (when introduced); waitlist email you choose to send
  • Contract — if you later subscribe to Pro and billing is enabled

6. Retention

Local ledger data: until you delete it or clear browser storage. Hosting logs: according to the host’s policy. Waitlist mailto messages you send: retained in email as ordinary correspondence.

7. Your rights (GDPR / Loi Informatique et Libertés)

You may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr). For data that exists only in your browser, use the in-app Delete control or clear site data. For emails you sent us, contact hello@sliply.app.

8. International transfers

CDNs and hosts may process data outside the EEA. Prefer EU-friendly Cloudflare settings and document SCCs / transfer tools when you go live with analytics or ads.

9. Children

Sliply is intended for business / adult use, not for children under 16.

10. Changes

We may update this notice; the “Last updated” date will change. Material changes for ads or Pro billing should be highlighted.
